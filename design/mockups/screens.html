When can the executors safely distribute the Whitfield estate?

Not before 6 months after the grant, and only once the statutory notice has expired. Whitfield has no grant yet, so there is no distribution date to diarise.1

  • Distributing after 6 months from the grant, with no notice of a family provision claim, protects the executors (s 99A).2
  • The s 33 notice to creditors and claimants must also have expired. It hasn’t been published.3
  • Thomas Whitfield is a likely claimant, and the file note of 14 Aug suggests he is thinking about it.4

If the grant issues in mid-November, the earliest safe date is mid-May 2027.
