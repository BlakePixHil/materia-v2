Get deadline alerts on this device

Materia can tell this device when a critical deadline is close or nobody has acknowledged it, even when Materia is closed. Alerts name the matter number, never the client.

Install Materia to receive alerts

On iPhone and iPad, deadline alerts come only to Materia on your Home Screen. Install it, open it from there, and turn alerts on.
