Claude connector

Use materia from your own Claude: ask about your matters, deadlines, tasks and time, and have Claude suggest tasks, file notes and time entries that someone accepts here.

For the practice

Off until you turn it on. Turning it off ends every connection at once.

Let people connect Claude
Each person connects their own Claude account, and it sees only what they can see in materia.
Let Claude suggest changes
Tasks, file notes and time entries wait on the matter until someone accepts them. When off, Claude only reads.
What people read through Claude goes into their own Claude account and is processed by Anthropic outside Australia, under that account’s terms. Ask them to use the practice’s Claude Team or Enterprise accounts, not personal ones.

Connect Claude

Add materia to Claude once. Claude then asks you to sign in here and allow it.

https://app.haleprice.example.com.au/mcp
  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it materia, paste the address above and choose Add.
  3. Choose Connect, sign in to materia and allow Claude.
In Claude Code, run claude mcp add --transport http materia https://app.haleprice.example.com.au/mcp, then /mcp to sign in.
Connect the Claude account the practice gave you (Team or Enterprise), not a personal one. Privileged matters and the text of documents are never shared, and every request is in the audit log.
