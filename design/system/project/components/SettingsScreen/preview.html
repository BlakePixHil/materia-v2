Clerk and AI

Choose what Clerk can read and do for Hale & Price. These settings apply to everyone; people can turn individual features off for themselves under Preferences.

What Clerk can read

Clerk only reads what is on here, and only within matters the person asking can open.

Matter documents
Wills, certificates, statements, correspondence and file notes filed to a matter.
Email
Emails filed to a matter or exchanged with a matter’s contacts. Personal mail is never read.
Calendar
Appointments and court dates, to suggest deadlines and capture time.
Phone call logs
Call times and durations from your phone system, for time capture.
Trust account ledger
Needs the trust account integration.

What Clerk can do

Everything Clerk produces arrives as a suggestion or a draft.

Morning brief
A summary of what needs you, on Today at 7:30 am.
Suggest tasks and deadlines
From new documents, emails and the rule sets.
Capture time
Draft time entries from email, calendar, calls and documents.
Draft entries for review
Draft documents
Draft letters, affidavits and court documents for a person to review.
Send, file or bill
Clerk never sends email, files with a registry or issues a bill. A person always does.
