# Collection notice

This notice is given under Australian Privacy Principle 5. It has two parts: what we collect from the people who use Materia, and wording a law firm can give its own clients about its use of Materia.

## Part 1: for people who use Materia

**Who is collecting.** [Operator legal name] (ABN [ABN]), which operates Materia. Contact our privacy officer at [Privacy contact email].

**What we collect.** Your name, work email address, password (stored only in a protected form), your role and firm, and the professional details your firm adds (such as your title, admission number and rates). When you sign in we record the time, the method, a coarse device and browser description and whether you used a second factor. We do not record your IP address in your sign-in history.

**How we collect it.** From you when you accept an invitation or set up your firm, from your firm's owner or administrators, from Google if you choose to sign in with Google, and from your use of Materia.

**Why.** To set up and secure your account, sign you in, apply your firm's security settings, send the notifications you choose, keep an audit trail of changes, support you, and meet our legal obligations. If you don't give us your name and email address, we can't give you an account.

**Who we share it with.** The service providers that run parts of Materia, listed with their locations in the [Sub-processor list](subprocessors.md); the other members of your firm, as your firm's settings allow; and anyone the law requires us to. Your information is stored in Australia, but some providers are overseas: email notifications are sent through Resend in the United States, and Google's sign-in, bot-protection and push services run on Google's global infrastructure.

**Your rights.** Our [Privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) explains how to access or correct your information and how to complain, including to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

## Part 2: wording for firms to give their clients

Materia does not collect information from a firm's clients directly: the firm collects it and keeps its client files in Materia. Each firm is responsible for its own collection notices to its clients. A firm may wish to include wording like the following in its client engagement documents or privacy policy. It is a suggestion only; the firm should adapt it to its own practice and obtain its own advice.

> We keep our client files, including your personal information and documents, in Materia, a practice management system operated for us by [Operator legal name]. Materia stores our files in Australia. It sends email notifications to our staff through a provider in the United States, and some of its sign-in and security services run on Google's global infrastructure. If we use its optional AI assistant, Clerk, relevant parts of your file are processed by our chosen AI provider outside Australia under our agreement with that provider, and a lawyer reviews anything it produces. Materia handles your information only on our instructions and keeps it confidential. Our privacy policy explains how to access or correct your information and how to complain.
