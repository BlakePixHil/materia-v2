# Privacy policy

## Who we are

Materia is a practice management tool for Australian wills and estates lawyers. It is operated by [Operator legal name] (ABN [ABN]) of [Registered address] ("we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), including the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

We handle personal information in line with the APPs whether or not the small business exemption would otherwise apply to us.

## The two kinds of information we hold

**Information about the people who use Materia.** When a law firm signs up, the people at the firm who use Materia (owners, lawyers, paralegals and other staff, "users") give us information so we can run their accounts. For this information we decide how it is handled, and this policy applies to it directly.

**Information firms keep in Materia about their clients and matters.** Firms use Materia to keep their client files: details of clients, deceased persons, executors, beneficiaries, other parties and witnesses, and the documents, notes, deadlines and bills on each matter ("client information"). The firm collects this information and decides what it is used for. We handle it only on the firm's behalf and on its instructions, under our [Terms of service](terms-of-service.md) and [Data processing addendum](dpa.md). The firm's own privacy policy and collection notices tell its clients how the firm handles their information. If you are a firm's client, please contact the firm first about your information; we will help the firm respond.

## What we collect about users

- **Account details:** your name, work email address, password (we only ever store a protected form of it, never the password itself), your role in the firm and which firm you belong to.
- **Sign-in and security details:** when you signed in, the sign-in method, a coarse description of your device and browser (for example "Chrome on macOS"), whether you used a second factor, your authenticator enrolment and your one-time recovery codes (stored as protected hashes). We do not record your IP address in your sign-in history.
- **Professional details your firm adds:** your title, practising certificate or admission number, initials, charge-out rates, budgets and supervisor.
- **Preferences and activity:** your settings (theme, notifications, start page), saved views, recently opened matters, your running timer, and records of the changes you make (the audit log).
- **Push notification tokens**, if you allow notifications on a device.
- **Messages to us**, if you contact us for support or send feedback.

## What client information firms keep in Materia

Depending on how a firm uses Materia, client information can include names, dates of birth and death, contact details and addresses, family and other relationships, assets, liabilities and distributions of an estate, financial records, identity documents, wills and other legal documents, court documents, file notes, time and billing records, and correspondence. It can include **sensitive information**, such as health information in a capacity dispute, and much of it is subject to **legal professional privilege**. We treat all client information as confidential and potentially privileged.

## How we use personal information

We use users' information to:

- provide Materia: create and secure accounts, sign you in, apply your firm's security settings (such as requiring two-step sign-in), and show you your firm's work;
- send the notifications you have chosen (in the app, by email or by push);
- keep Materia secure and reliable: detect misuse, investigate incidents and keep an audit trail;
- answer your support requests and improve Materia; and
- meet our legal obligations.

We use client information only to provide Materia to the firm that entered it, as the firm directs. We do not use client information for our own purposes, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

## Clerk, the AI assistant

Materia includes Clerk, an AI assistant that can answer questions about a matter, suggest tasks and deadlines, sort and read documents, and draft text. Clerk is off until a firm's owner or administrator turns it on and saves the firm's own API key for the AI provider. When a firm uses Clerk, the parts of a matter needed for the request (the question, key facts, and relevant parts of documents and notes) are sent to the AI provider the firm has chosen, under the firm's own agreement with that provider. The AI provider currently supported is Anthropic, which processes requests outside Australia. Clerk only proposes; a person decides. See the [Sub-processor list](subprocessors.md).

## Where personal information is stored

Materia's data, including client information, documents and backups, is stored in Google Cloud data centres in Australia: Sydney (`australia-southeast1`) for the live service and Melbourne (`australia-southeast2`) for backups. Search indexes for documents are also built in Sydney.

Some information leaves Australia:

- **Email notifications** are sent through Resend, Inc. in the United States, which handles the recipient's address and the email's subject and body. Email subjects never contain matter details, and emails never contain privileged content.
- **Some Google services that Materia relies on are global**, rather than tied to an Australian region: sign-in (Identity Platform), bot protection (App Check with reCAPTCHA Enterprise), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and feature settings (Remote Config). They handle account and device information, not client files.
- **Clerk requests**, when a firm turns Clerk on, are processed by the firm's chosen AI provider outside Australia (see above).
- **Integrations a firm or user connects**, such as Xero, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, receive the information the firm sends them, wherever those services store it.

The [Sub-processor list](subprocessors.md) names every service provider, what it does, what information it handles and where. We take reasonable steps under APP 8 to make sure overseas recipients handle personal information consistently with the APPs.

## Who we disclose personal information to

We disclose personal information only:

- to the service providers on the [Sub-processor list](subprocessors.md), so they can provide their part of Materia;
- within a firm, as the firm's users and settings allow (for example, members of a firm see each other's names, and only some roles see privileged material);
- to integrations a firm or user chooses to connect;
- where the law requires or authorises it. If we receive a request from a government agency or court for a firm's client information, we refer it to the firm unless the law prevents us, and we assert privilege on the firm's behalf where it applies; and
- to a buyer of our business, under obligations to protect it as this policy does.

## How we protect personal information

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, change or disclosure (APP 11). They include:

- data kept in Australia, encrypted in transit and at rest;
- each firm's information kept separate from every other firm's, with access checked on every request;
- two-step sign-in, which firms can require, session time limits and remote sign-out;
- access by role, with privileged material shown only to the roles allowed to see it;
- an audit log of changes, kept for seven years;
- malware scanning of uploaded files before anyone can open them;
- no personal information or document text in our technical logs; and
- daily backups held in a second Australian region, and tested restores.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it, and then delete or de-identify it. Our [Retention policy](retention-policy.md) sets out the periods, including how a firm's own retention settings for closed matters work and when deleted information leaves our backups.

## Accessing and correcting your information

You can see and update most of your account details in Materia. You can also ask us for access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you (APPs 12 and 13) by contacting us below. We will respond within 30 days, and we will tell you why if we can't do what you ask.

If your request is about client information a firm keeps in Materia, contact that firm. We will help the firm respond to it.

## Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will act under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme: we assess suspected breaches promptly (within 30 days at the latest), contain them, notify the firms affected without undue delay, and notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) and the people affected where the scheme requires it.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Materia uses only what it needs to work: your browser's storage keeps you signed in, remembers your preferences and holds a copy of your firm's data so the app works offline. Signing out clears it. Materia does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## Changes to this policy

We will publish any change to this policy in Materia with a new version number and date. If a change is significant, we will tell firms' owners before it takes effect.
