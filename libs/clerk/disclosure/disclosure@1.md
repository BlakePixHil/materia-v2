## Who processes it

Clerk runs on Anthropic's Claude models, through your firm's own Anthropic API account (the key saved on this page). Your firm's agreement with Anthropic covers that processing, and your firm pays Anthropic directly.

## Where

Anthropic processes Clerk requests outside Australia. Requests are routed globally and Anthropic stores account data in the United States. Everything else in Materia, including Clerk's answers and suggestions once they come back, is stored in Google Cloud in Sydney.

## What is sent

Only what the feature you use needs, and only from matters the person asking can open:

- the question or task;
- the matter's key facts, such as parties, key dates, open tasks and deadlines;
- the parts of documents and notes that are relevant, or a whole document when Clerk sorts or reads it.

Nothing from another firm is ever sent, and nothing is sent for a feature your firm has turned off.

## How long it is kept

Anthropic keeps API requests and responses for a limited time under its retention policy and its commercial terms, which don't allow using them to train its models. Your firm can ask Anthropic for zero data retention. In Materia, Clerk's conversations and suggestions are kept with the matter file and destroyed with it.

## Privilege

Privileged documents are used only when the person asking is allowed to see them (owners, admins and lawyers). Anything Clerk produces from privileged material is marked privileged too.

## Who decides

Clerk proposes and a person decides. It never sends email, files with a court or registry, or issues a bill. Practitioners remain responsible for the advice they give, and Clerk's answers carry citations so they can be checked.
